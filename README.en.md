## 📖 Introduction

This document formally records the history and evolution of **Roleplay Capitalista**, preserving its identity, leadership transitions, and structural milestones throughout the years.

---

## 🕰️ Foundation — 2016

Roleplay Capitalista was founded in mid-2016.

Since its inception, the server adopted a seasonal operational model, typically reopening during:

- 📆 November to February  
- 📆 June to August  

This cyclical structure became part of the project's identity, generating anticipation and strong community engagement.

---

## 🗺️ Structural Evolution

Throughout its existence, the server operated across:

- 4 different maps  
- 2 exclusive custom maps developed in recent seasons  

The custom maps were created under the pseudonym **SkrUnlimited (Trevor)**, marking a phase of technical maturity and creative control.

The current map represents the most refined and recent version of the project.

---

## 🎙️ Community Communication

Before 2018, the project used **TeamSpeak** as its primary communication platform.

With the rise of Discord as the global standard, the project transitioned accordingly, resulting in:

- 2 official Discord servers over time  
  - 1 archived  
  - 1 currently active  

This transition represented a significant modernization in community management.
